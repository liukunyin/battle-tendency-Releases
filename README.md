挑选你喜欢的角色，让他们在同一座擂台上交手。观看自动对战、安排队伍和连战，或在「碰撞时机」模式中亲自决定角色的反弹方向。

当前版本 **0.3**，共 **64 名角色**，包含《JOJO的奇妙冒险》《Fate/Grand Order》《超时空辉夜姬！》等作品中的角色。

### 玩法

- **组队对战**：搭配角色，进行队伍混战。
- **大乱斗**：全员混战，看看谁能留到最后。
- **连战模式**：逐个挑战对手。
- **团队车轮战**：败者退场，胜者继续守擂。
- **碰撞时机**：操控一名角色与 AI 对决。开战前选择位置和方向；碰撞后进入子弹时间，用鼠标或触摸指定反弹方向。

## English

Choose your favorite fighters and bring them into the same arena. Watch automatic battles, build teams and gauntlets, or steer your fighter's rebounds yourself in **Bounce Timing** mode.

Version **0.3** features **64 fighters**, including characters from *JoJo's Bizarre Adventure*, *Fate/Grand Order*, *Cosmic Princess Kaguya!*, and other series.

### Game modes

- **Team Battle**: Build teams and watch them clash.
- **Battle Royale**: A free-for-all. See who survives to the end.
- **Gauntlet**: Challenge opponents one by one.
- **Team Relay**: The loser leaves; the winner stays to face the next fighter.
- **Bounce Timing**: Control one fighter against the AI. Set your starting position and direction, then use the mouse or touch controls to aim your rebounds during bullet time after an impact.
